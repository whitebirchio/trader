# Momentum vs VTI, 2018–2026

> Backtest for [#49](https://github.com/whitebirchio/trader/issues/49), run 2026-11-08 from Alpaca's adjusted daily bars ([plan](../../tasks/momentum-quality-plan.md)). Decided on 2018–2023, with 2024 to May 2026 held out under a promotion rule fixed before any results. The summary is hand-written from the tables below, which `npm run research:momentum -w @trader/core -- --report ../../docs/research/momentum-vs-vti-2026-11.md` regenerates.

## Bottom line

**Buying the strongest recent performers didn't beat VTI, so no momentum variant passes the promotion rule. But the weakest recent performers kept losing badly, in both periods: momentum works as a signal of what *not* to buy.**

1. **The top decile doesn't pass.** In-sample, the top decile trailed VTI for every variant (12-1: −0.33% a month; above the 200-day average: −0.30%; 6-1: −0.50%). 12-1 did better on the held-out months (+0.52% a month before costs, ±0.82%), but the rule needed both periods to agree, which is why it was fixed in advance.
2. **The bottom decile is the robust result.** Stocks with the worst past 12 months trailed VTI by **−1.45% a month in-sample and −3.65% a month held out**, losing to VTI in 70% and 83% of months. The 6-1 bottom decile looks the same (−1.68% and −3.32%).
3. **The spread is real, but Trader can't short it.** Top minus bottom: +1.11% a month in-sample and +4.18% held out (about 4 standard errors). That matches decades of published work, and it's almost all on the short side.
4. **It agrees with the two earlier studies.** Buying after a big drop lost money after insider buying (#29), after an earnings crash (#48), and now as a general price pattern.

## What it means for Trader

- **Avoid buying bottom-decile momentum** (the worst past-12-month returns among tradable stocks) unless a thesis explains specifically why the decline is over, with a dated catalyst. This is the evidence-backed version of "don't catch falling knives", and it applies to much of the live book's dip-buying.
- **Don't add a momentum lead signal or revive the systematic sleeve on this evidence.** The top decile didn't beat VTI in-sample, so it fails the rule that was set before any results.
- **Recent strength is fine supporting context, not a reason to buy.** The top decile's held-out months were positive, but they don't meet the bar.

## Caveats

- **Survivorship:** the universe is symbols with cached bars, mostly companies trading today. The missing delistings were mostly losers, so the true bottom decile was likely *worse* than shown (the avoid signal is conservative), and every bucket is flattered against VTI.
- **Overlap:** 60-day windows from consecutive months overlap, so their standard errors understate the uncertainty. The 20-day figures are the primary measure.
- **Equal-weighted and monthly,** with no taxes. Costs enter only through the promotion rule.

<!-- generated by npm run research:momentum: everything below is regenerated -->

**Data:** 11,035 symbols with cached Alpaca bars (mostly companies trading today, plus the insider-event names from #29); 95 month-end formations from 2018-07-31 to 2026-05-29. **In-sample:** formations before 2024-01-01. **Held out:** from 2024-01-01.

Each month-end, the stocks that pass risk's filters at the next close are ranked by the signal into deciles (10 = highest). A decile's result is the mean, over its stocks, of the return minus VTI's from the first close after formation. The statistics run over the **monthly series** of those means: the mean, its standard error, and the share of months the decile beat VTI. 60-day windows from consecutive months overlap, so their standard errors understate the uncertainty.

**Promotion rule (fixed before any results):** the top decile's mean 20-day excess must beat VTI in-sample, and on the held-out months after a 0.2% round-trip cost.
